Move Catalog

13-point update item 13: every individual move in the game, defined once here — edit a move's stats and it updates everywhere it's assigned below (an affinity baseline, a species's signature move, or a deity's signature override), no more hunting down the same move in three tables. Kind picks the slot a move can fill: Power (no swiftness shave) or Control (hits softer, shaves the defender's Swiftness for the rest of the fight) need an Affinity (used by the Element Wheel); Basic is the universal fallback strike and has none. Archetype (0086) tags what the move is FOR, independent of Kind: Damage (the default — deals its Power as a hit), Support (heals/buffs the caster — set Power to 0 for a pure heal that deals no damage at all), or Tank (defensive — shield_flat works standalone now instead of only riding on a damage roll). A move can also carry a real triggered effect on top of its damage: lifesteal_pct (heal this % of the hit's damage), stun_pct (this % chance to skip the target's next action), crit_bonus_pct (+this many points of crit chance on this hit), burn_flat (ongoing damage/round for the rest of the fight), poison_pct (a second, independent DoT — this % of the target's max Vitality/round), weaken_pct (cuts the target's own Might by this % for the rest of the fight), vulnerability_pct (the target takes this % more damage from everything for the rest of the fight), shield_flat (self-buff — absorbs your own next this-much total damage taken), heal_flat (0086 — heal the caster this flat amount, no damage dealt), or heal_pct (0086 — heal the caster this % of their own max Vitality, no damage dealt). Role is just a label for where a move is meant to be used — nothing reads it, so any move (including a brand new "custom" one) can be assigned anywhere a dropdown below lets you pick one. Particle (0070) picks which burst sprite plays when this move lands in Campaign, Gauntlet, and PvP — leave it "— affinity default —" to keep the automatic look every other move of that affinity already has, or pick a specific one to make this move stand out.

Battle Settings (0145)

Every number below used to be a hardcoded constant duplicated (often with slightly different values) across the combat, pvp, world-boss, circle, and mage edge functions — five different crit formulas, the same multiplier typed out ten separate times, no admin control at all. Now they're one shared, admin-editable row every one of those functions reads fresh on each fight. Saving here changes live gameplay immediately, no redeploy needed. Deliberately NOT included: the Element Wheel (its own tab above), Moves/Blessings/Gear stat bonuses (their own tabs), Energy & Tickets and Invasion Settings' economy fields (their own tabs), and the Circle roster's cosmetic Power Rating display formula, which is intentionally kept separate from the real invasion combat formula below so a display feature can never silently shift invasion balance.

Crit & Ward — the core resolveHit() formula (Gauntlet, PvP, World Boss, Circle Dragon)

Crit chance = min(Global cap, min(Base cap, Cunning ÷ Cunning divisor) + any aura crit bonus%). A crit multiplies raw damage by the Crit multiplier; every hit then subtracts Defender Ward × Ward mitigation coefficient, floored at Min damage floor.

Status-effect caps (auras)

Dodge and Damage Reduction are each capped by their own %. Weaken caps how much of the target's effective Might a weaken_pct stack can strip. Vulnerability has no cap in the shipped defaults — leave blank to keep it uncapped, or set a number to cap it for the first time.

Shared opponent-AI move-selection heuristic

Used by every NPC-controlled side (Gauntlet foes, PvP, World Boss, Circle Dragon) when picking a move each turn: below this HP% it will consider healing, at this chance it actually does; otherwise it falls back to a Basic Strike at this flat chance before scoring its remaining options.

Round caps

Flat reward amounts

Not already covered by a dedicated payout table (pvp_payout_rules, Universal Loot, etc.) — just the flat XP/Essence-per-win amounts.

World Boss & Circle Dragon

Shared cooldown after either shared-pool boss defeats your guardian. The three kinds of opponent with no real Cunning stat each get their own counter-crit knob instead: World Boss is a flat chance; Circle Dragon scales with the dragon's own Tier (chance = min(Cap, Tier × Coefficient)); Mage Duel's monster is flat too.

Mage Duel's own damage formula

Structurally separate from every other system's Might × move.power shape: raw = (Might × Might weight + Swiftness × Swiftness weight + Cunning × Cunning weight) × Baseline multiplier × the shared ± variance roll below × the Element Wheel multiplier. The Art lean (e.g. Pyromancy leans Might) adds this Leaned-stat bonus % on top of whichever stat it favors, before this formula runs.

Shared ± variance RNG roll

Circle invasion's power roll and Mage Duel's own and its monster's damage roll all independently hardcoded the same "±15%" shape — this is that one shared knob (e.g. 15 = ±15%, rolling somewhere between 85% and 115% of the base value).

Circle invasion's real combat formula

guardianPowerScore() — the formula that actually decides who wins an invasion: Might × Might weight + Ward × Ward weight + Cunning × Cunning weight + Swiftness × Swiftness weight + Vitality × Vitality weight (Vitality's weight ships at 0, i.e. unused, but is here in case you ever want it counted). This is deliberately separate from the Circle roster's cosmetic Power Rating display formula, which is NOT on this page and NOT affected by anything below.
